## Narration
Local text-to-speech output (macOS `say` or the configured local engine) is generated per episode and is not stored in `assets/`. Check the licence of any voice you choose for commercial use; ElevenLabs output follows your ElevenLabs plan.

## Artwork
Mascot, props, the illustration library (`src/visuals`) and the full-bleed scene objects (`src/fullbleed`) are drawn procedurally in code (SVG / CSS gradients). Original work; no image files from third parties.
